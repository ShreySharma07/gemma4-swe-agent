# Citation verification: research_deliverables/{literature_review.md, references.bib}, docs/literature_2025_2026.md

Checked 2026-10-22. Method: arXiv export API (ID lookup + title search), alphaXiv full-text queries, arXiv PDFs grepped locally, web search. "LR#n" = row n of the literature_review.md matrix (section 2); "DOC" = docs/literature_2025_2026.md; "BIB" = references.bib only.

Status key: VERIFIED = title + ID + key claim match. PARTIAL = paper exists, but the ID, authors, title, venue or quoted number differ. NOT FOUND = no trace after 2 or more searches.

**Headline:** the 2025–26 papers are almost all real. But the literature_review.md matrix (and the bib copied from it) **attached wrong arXiv IDs to 7 papers and invented author lists for about 20**. Many of its quoted numbers also don't appear in the papers. The docs/ file is much cleaner: all of its 15 arXiv IDs resolve to the right titles, and most of its numbers check out.
